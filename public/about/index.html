The Founder

George Constantin

Spatial engineer. Founder of Polyflow.

Polyflow began with a single conviction. The web has a third dimension, and most studios pretend it doesn't exist. Between the flat layouts the industry settled for and the spatial work browsers can already render, there is open ground. A foundation waiting to be built on.

That ground is where Polyflow stands. Est. 2023 in Bucharest, Romania, the studio crafts permanent spatial assets for ambitious brands, luxury maisons, and high-growth tech firms across Europe. Three commitments anchor every project. Depth as brand equity. Performance as foundation. Craft as the visible signature.

Background

George's path to spatial engineering started in computer science. Algorithms, data structures, GPU architecture. The creative layer came after, never instead. The approach is engineering-first. Understand the hardware, then shape the experience inside its weight. Work that reads as ambitious because it is engineered to be permanent.

Core disciplines span the full spatial pipeline:

  • Scene architecture. Scene graph design, camera systems, and render pipelines built to scale.
  • Custom shading. Hand-written vertex and fragment programs for effects no library can deliver.
  • Scroll choreography. Scroll-driven camera sequences and frame-perfect timeline composition.
  • Performance engineering. Draw call budgets, instanced rendering, and mobile GPU discipline.
  • Component architecture. Modular spatial systems for projects where structure matters as much as visuals.

The Polyflow Method

Most studios sell creative websites. Polyflow builds Digital Landmarks. The distinction matters because 3D on the web carries a reputation. Slow loads, broken mobile, fans spinning. Polyflow exists to retire that reputation, one project at a time.

The method rests on three principles:

  • Precision over volume. One project crafted exceptionally outweighs five delivered adequately. Each engagement receives the full depth of the studio's attention.
  • Performance as foundation. 60 FPS on iOS Safari is the baseline, never the ambition. Every build ships with an engineered mobile path.
  • Technology in service of narrative. Spatial depth exists to elevate the story. When a flat section communicates better, it stays flat.

Location
