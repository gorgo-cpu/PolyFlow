3D Website Design

Spatial experiences for ambitious brands that refuse to stay flat.

A spatial website is not decoration. It is a permanent brand asset. When every competitor sits on a templated grid, depth becomes leverage. The visitor lingers, the brand registers, and the price you can charge climbs with the dwell time. For luxury maisons and high-growth tech firms, that depth is the difference between a website and a landmark.

Polyflow designs and builds spatial websites from the foundation up. No templates, no drag-and-drop builders, no plugins masquerading as depth. Each project is bespoke, engineered, and tuned to ship at 60 FPS on every device, including iOS Safari, where most spatial work quietly fails.

What a Spatial Website Includes

Scroll-Driven Camera

The scroll becomes a cinematic path through space. Content reveals itself spatially, not vertically, and the brand gains a sequence the visitor remembers.

Custom Shading

Hand-written shaders for surfaces that no library delivers. Holographic skins, gradient flows, and particle fields shaped to the brand's exact register.

3D Model Integration

Product models, brand objects, and bespoke geometry loaded as optimized assets with refined lighting and environment maps.

Performance Engineering

Draw call budgets, instanced rendering, mobile GPU discipline. The site holds 60 FPS, or it does not ship.

The Technical Foundation

Every spatial website Polyflow ships rests on a proven, engineered foundation:

  • Scene architecture. Composition, camera systems, and a render pipeline crafted for maintainability and weight.
  • Custom shading. Vertex and fragment programs written for the brand, not borrowed from a material library.
  • Scroll choreography. Frame-perfect timeline control with scroll-driven sequencing and cinematic pacing.
  • Smooth scroll layer. Engineered scroll physics that protect the depth of the experience on every device.
  • Post-processing. Bloom, chromatic aberration, film grain, and tone mapping composed into a single refined render.

We choose between vanilla and component-based architecture per project. The decision is technical, never ideological, and always anchored to performance.

The Performance Guarantee

Every Polyflow project ships with a 60 FPS guarantee on mobile and desktop. This is not a marketing line. It is an engineering constraint embedded in the build from day one. Each experience carries an engineered mobile path that adapts particle density, disables heavier passes, and matches the device's true capability.

Pixel ratios are clamped, draw calls are budgeted, and frames are profiled. When a feature cannot hold 60 FPS on a mid-range phone, it is refined, redesigned, or removed. No "best viewed on desktop" disclaimers.
